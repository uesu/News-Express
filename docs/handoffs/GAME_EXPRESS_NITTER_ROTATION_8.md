## Cost note (answers the "is 4 fine?" question)

Unchanged mechanics: 4 parallel probes per handle per run, the batch stops once the two
highest-ranked answer (1.5 s grace for a straggler, cancelled otherwise), reserve seats capped at
2 in flight across games. Six handles → 24 requests per run, 12 of them the quorum; nothing reaches
wave 2 on a healthy run. What changes: all 24 now land on the teapawt origin (scraping-permitted),
whereas before 12 went to teapawt and 6 each to meowing/miningtcup. A teapawt-origin outage costs
one `NITTER_TIMEOUT` (12 s) before wave 2 — also teapawt + mizuki — and a second before the
independent bench in wave 3. If you ever want to trim, the knob is `NITTER_BATCH` in
`gamexpress/sources/twitter.py` (2 → a healthy run costs 12 requests); not changed here.
